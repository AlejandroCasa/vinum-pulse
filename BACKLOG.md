# Technical Backlog & Architecture Roadmap — Vinum Pulse

This file tracks prioritized engineering improvements, features, and completed milestones for **Vinum Pulse**.

## 🎯 Prioritized Backlog (Sprint 2026-W47)

### P0 — High-Impact Gastronomic & Event Planning
- [ ] **[event-planner]** Interactive Evening & 3-Course Menu Sommelier Planner (`POST /api/v1/pairings/menu-plan`): Let any user select an occasion (*Cena Romántica*, *Barbacoa con Amigos*, *Comida Familiar*, *Tapas Gourmet*) and a 3-course menu (*Entrante + Principal + Postre/Queso*) within a target budget. Automatically compute optimal wine serving progression (light-to-full structure), plain-language serving tips (*"45 min in fridge before serving"*, *"Uncork 30 min early"*), vintage peak status semaphore, and a **Printable Sommelier Menu Card** for guests (Impact: 5/5 | Effort: M)

### P1 — UX Polish & Component Interactions
- [ ] **[ui/la-carte-accordion]** **"La Carte" Collapsible Sections & Auto-Collapse (`app/ui.py` + `app/sommelier.py`)**: Group dishes into expandable accordion sections (*Entrantes y Cuchara*, *Ensaladas y Huerta*, *Tapas y Pintxos*, *Embutidos y Quesos*, *Pescados y Mariscos*, *Arroces*, *Carnes y Caza*, *Postres y Dulces*, *Platos Vegetarianos y Setas*); clicking a section expands its dishes, and selecting a dish auto-collapses the list into a compact summary bar to bring **"En Bodega"** closer (Impact: 5/5 | Effort: S)
- [ ] **[ui/wine-hook-expand]** **Concise Wine Hook & Interactive Tasting Expansion (`app/schemas.py` + `app/sommelier.py` + `app/ui.py`)**: Add a consumer-facing short `tagline` on wine cards to capture attention immediately, expanding smoothly on click/hover to reveal the full `tasting_notes` (Impact: 4/5 | Effort: S)
- [ ] **[ui/bottle-label-visual]** **Editorial Bottle & Label Visual (`app/schemas.py` + `app/ui.py`)**: Render an elegant bottle/label visual on the pairing card (deterministic editorial SVG bottle silhouette by wine style — Bordeaux, Burgundy, Sherry, Sparkling — with typographic D.O./Winery/Vintage label, plus optional `image_url` support) (Impact: 4/5 | Effort: S)

### P2 — Kubernetes Orchestration & Weighted Algorithms
- [ ] **[k8s]** Declarative Kubernetes Manifests (`k8s/`): Create `deployment.yaml` and `service.yaml` with hardened `securityContext` (`runAsNonRoot: true`, `UID 10001`, `readOnlyRootFilesystem: true`, probes on `/healthz`) for local `k3s` (Impact: 4/5 | Effort: S)
